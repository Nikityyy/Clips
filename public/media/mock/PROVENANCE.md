# Bundled mock media

The portrait fixtures are synthetic demo images generated for Clips. They depict one fictional person, not a real individual, and are not Google Flow output. Every portrait PNG embeds the exact prompt used to generate that raster; `generation-prompt.txt` contains the source-sheet prompt for `mara-02.png` through `mara-06.png`. `mara-01.png` was regenerated individually from the approved composition crop to preserve its pose and crop.

`mock-video.mp4` is a short local motion sample derived from portrait `mara-06.png`. It exists to exercise video playback and library workflows; it is not a generated Flow video. Mock jobs use these fixtures locally and consume no provider credits.
